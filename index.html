Visualizer concepts: three directions on Turbo

Waveform lab: six motion ideas for the waveform →

Each concept has a YouTube 16:9 version and an Instagram story 9:16 version, both reacting live to Turbo from bar 88. Press Play inside each frame. Each uses the two waveforms picked from the lab: flowing ridges (default) and pump bars, switchable with the Waveform button under each frame. The analysis is precomputed, so the motion lands on the beat. Play one at a time; each runs its own audio.

Liquid Light

The cover's colours flow like a 60s oil-light show (WebGL shader). Bass deepens the warp and glow; highs add fine sparkle. Glassy selectr bars, teal to gold as it plays.

The art as particles

The illustration rebuilt from about 19,500 glowing particles sampled from the art. It scatters and re-forms every 8 bars; kicks send a light ripple through it; sparks rise off the tallest bars.

Depth and light

A slow 3D drift through the cover (three.js): parallax art, light shafts from above, floating bokeh. Bars are glowing columns on a reflective floor. Kick drives the light shafts, highs the bokeh.
